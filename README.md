# The Restoration of All Things

An interactive, illustrated single-page app on the biblical theme of *apokatastasis*, "the restoration of all things" (Acts 3:21). It traces how God keeps stooping into people's condition and culture to restore them, shows Jesus as the pinnacle of that restoration, and follows how it continues through the Body of Christ. It also sets the relational gospel of union beside the legal frame.

## Open it

There is no build step. Open `index.html` in any modern browser, or serve the folder:

```bash
python3 -m http.server 8000
```

## Chapters

| Chapter | What you can do |
| --- | --- |
| **Home** | Drag the "Scattered → Restored" slider and watch fragments gather into four rings around the cross. An animated "shape of the story" diagram shows humanity's line falling and God's line coming down until they meet at the cross and rise together. |
| **The Story** | Step through 13 movements from Creation to New Creation, each with its own animated illustration (the sea parting, stars over Abraham's tent, the stone rolling from the tomb, flames at Pentecost). For each: what broke, how God came down, what He restored, how it points to Christ, and what it means for you today. Press **Play the story** to auto-advance. |
| **God Stoops** | See "God's changing address" from Eden to "all in all". Browse 18 examples of God accommodating to a culture (covenant rituals, Egyptian dreams, the Magi's star, the Areopagus, Pentecost). |
| **Four Spheres** | Tap a ring (with animated ripples) to see where that relationship broke, what its restoration looks like today, and every narrative that restores it. A bar chart counts the library's stories per sphere and testament. |
| **Jesus** | The six-step arc of Christ's work (incarnation to Pentecost) read as union, plus 12 flip cards from shadow to substance, and an animated flow diagram from five fractures, through Christ, to five restorations. |
| **Two Lenses** | 14 topics (sin, the cross, salvation, law, holiness, wrath, the prodigal…) shown as the legal frame, the relational union reading, or side by side, with where the twist happens. Includes a short history of how the frame shifted and a self-check with an animated gauge. |
| **Hard Texts** | 55 harsh-sounding passages (wrath, hell, Gehenna, the lake of fire, the flood, Sodom, the conquest, Elisha's bears, the cross, Ananias and Sapphira…) each read through its Hebrew or Greek root words, its culture, and where the story goes next. Includes an animated word lens, a cycle diagram of how judgments end in restoration, a lexicon of 74 root words, and honest notes where a text stays hard. |
| **The Body** | An interactive map of 12 members connected to Christ the Head, and what restoration looks like in each sphere today. |
| **Narratives** | Searchable library of 82 restoration stories, filterable by sphere and testament. |
| **Life Today** | A whole day animated from dawn to night with eight moments lived through both lenses; nine real situations (failure, family rift, anxiety, grief…) rewritten from the legal script to the union script; an Acts 1:8 ripple diagram; and an "already / not yet" timeline. |
| **My Journey** | 16 practices across the four spheres, with a progress ring saved in your browser. |

## Colours and display

Use the palette button in the header to choose one of six schemes (Jordan River, Eden Garden, Resurrection Dawn, Pentecost Fire, True Vine, Sapphire Throne). Each has a light and dark version. You can also follow the system brightness or force light or dark, change text size, turn ambient particles on or off, let the page tint with each movement of the story, or let the palette cycle slowly through the schemes. Settings are remembered in your browser.

The sparkle button shows a random restoration story.

## Notes

- Quotations marked WEB are from the World English Bible (public domain); other references are summarised.
- The app respects `prefers-reduced-motion`.

# The Restoration of All Things

An interactive, illustrated single-page app on the biblical theme of *apokatastasis*, "the restoration of all things" (Acts 3:21). It traces how God keeps stooping into people's condition and culture to restore them, shows Jesus as the pinnacle of that restoration, and follows how it continues through the Body of Christ. It also sets the relational gospel of union beside the legal frame.

## Open it

There is no build step. Open `index.html` in any modern browser, or serve the folder:

```bash
python3 -m http.server 8000
```

## Chapters

| Chapter | What you can do |
| --- | --- |
| **Home** | Drag the "Scattered → Restored" slider and watch fragments gather into four rings around the cross. |
| **The Story** | Step through 13 movements from Creation to New Creation. For each one: what broke, how God came down, what He restored, and how it points to Christ. Arrow keys work. |
| **God Stoops** | See "God's changing address" from Eden to "all in all". Browse 18 examples of God accommodating to a culture (covenant rituals, Egyptian dreams, the Magi's star, the Areopagus, Pentecost). |
| **Four Spheres** | Tap the personal, family, culture or earth ring to see where that relationship broke and every narrative that restores it. |
| **Jesus** | The six-step arc of Christ's work (incarnation to Pentecost) read as union, plus 12 flip cards from shadow to substance. |
| **Two Lenses** | 14 topics (sin, the cross, salvation, law, holiness, wrath, the prodigal…) shown as the legal frame, the relational union reading, or side by side, with where the twist happens. Includes a short history of how the frame shifted. |
| **The Body** | An interactive map of 12 members connected to Christ the Head, and what restoration looks like in each sphere today. |
| **Narratives** | Searchable library of 82 restoration stories, filterable by sphere and testament. |
| **My Journey** | 16 practices across the four spheres, with a progress ring saved in your browser. |

## Colours and display

Use the palette button in the header to choose one of six schemes (Jordan River, Eden Garden, Resurrection Dawn, Pentecost Fire, True Vine, Sapphire Throne). Each has a light and dark version. You can also follow the system brightness or force light or dark, change text size, turn ambient particles on or off, let the page tint with each movement of the story, or let the palette cycle slowly through the schemes. Settings are remembered in your browser.

The sparkle button shows a random restoration story.

## Notes

- Quotations marked WEB are from the World English Bible (public domain); other references are summarised.
